Four starts are planned for 2027. One of them decides whether the season worked: KAT100 on 6 August, 75 km and 4,100 m of climbing, starting at midnight in Kitzbühel. Everything before it is either preparation or unfinished business.

## Why KAT100

Most races I've done were about holding a pace. KAT100 is about holding on: a midnight start, hours in the dark with a headlamp, and a finish in Fieberbrunn long after sunrise.

What draws me is the head, not the legs. I want to find out where my limit is when the effort lasts all night. An all-out 10k hurts, but it's over before you can start arguing with yourself. This one won't be.

The entry requirement is already done. I ran the [Rothaarsteig Marathon](/blog/rothaarsteig-2026) in October at zone 2 for one reason: the 50K UTMB index I need to start.

## Hamburg, 25 April: sub-3 without a marathon block

Berlin was supposed to be my sub-3. Up to the medical tent I was on pace, 4:15 per kilometre for 19 km. Then the race was over ([the full story](/blog/berlin-marathon-2026)). Hamburg is the rematch.

But Hamburg isn't the main event, and I won't pretend it is. There's no dedicated marathon build this winter. The plan is high volume for KAT100, with a few key sessions on top. My bet is that the volume carries me under three hours anyway. The shape was there this year; the race just didn't let me show it.

Sub-3:00 is the goal. On a good day, 2:55 is possible. We'll see.

## Frankfurt, 27 June: finishing what I started

Honest version: during and after [Kraichgau](/blog/ironman-kraichgau) I decided that triathlon isn't for me. My passion is running.

But I started endurance sport because I wanted to become an Ironman one day. So I'm going to finish that: 3.8 km swim, 180 km bike, 42.2 km run. The goal is the finish line, nothing more. KAT100 is six weeks later, so Frankfurt is a side quest and gets treated like one.

## Backyard ultra, from September

6.7 km every hour, on the hour, until you can't start the next loop. No race chosen yet. That depends a lot on how the first three go, and on what's left after KAT100.
