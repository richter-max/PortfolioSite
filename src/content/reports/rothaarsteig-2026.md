Three weeks after Berlin, a trail marathon in the Sauerland. Not a race for me.

I ran it with a friend, at zone 2 the whole way. We started together and finished together, in the rain, 9.5 °C and feeling colder. It was a lot of fun.

The real reason I was there: a finish in a UTMB-indexed race in the 50K category. That's the index I need to start at KAT100 in Kitzbühel next August, the main event of my 2027 season.

So this one was a ticket. Ticket booked.
