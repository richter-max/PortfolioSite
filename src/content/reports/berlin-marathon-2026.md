Official time 3:48. The goal was sub-3:00. That's the result, so it goes first.

## The plan

Sub-3 means 4:16 per kilometre for 42.195 km. I started in block C after a hard build, and on the morning I felt ready.

## Km 3.5

The start blocks in Berlin sort runners by expected finish time. There weren't enough staff at the entrances, and runners from much later blocks, K and further back, got into the front ones. One of them had climbed over a fence to start ahead of his block.

He was running somewhere around 8:00 to 9:00 per kilometre in a field moving at 4:15. Everyone was passing him. At km 3.5 he tried to get out of someone's way and stepped on my ankle.

## Km 3.5 to 19

The tendons around the ankle hurt until about km 7, then it eased off. The pace held. Up to the tent my watch shows 4:15 per kilometre on average, right on sub-3 pace, and apart from the ankle the body felt good.

From km 12 the pain came back. From km 17 it was hell. At km 19 I stepped off the course and into the medical tent.

## The tent

About 15 minutes. When I came out, sub-3 was gone and there were 23 km left. I was really frustrated, but I wanted to bring it home.

## Km 19 to 25

Walking and jogging, in turns. I was on FaceTime with a few people the whole way, just to get my head somewhere else. From km 22 I was jogging only. At km 24 I decided on 6:00 to 6:30 per kilometre to the finish. Nothing left to race for.

## Km 25 to the finish

At km 25 I passed a runner who was really suffering. I asked if she wanted me to pace her. I was thinking 6:00. She said 5:10.

So we ran 5:10, more or less. My watch has the last 17 km between 4:38 and 5:59 per kilometre, around 5:20 on average. We crossed the line together, talked for a minute, and went our separate ways.

Having a job again made the second half a lot easier for my head.

## What I take from it

You don't have everything in your own hands. However well you prepare, sometimes all you can do is make the best of what you get.

One thing I'd like to see change: more staff at the block entrances. A runner at 8:00 per kilometre in a block running 4:15 is a risk for him and for everyone around him.

## Next

Hamburg Marathon, 25 April 2027. Same goal.
